---

## 1. Product Vision

> **Build India's trusted AI-powered marketplace for discovering, comparing, and hiring photography and videography professionals, with a scalable foundation for international expansion.**

### Tagline
**Discover. Compare. Connect. Capture.**

---

## 2. Core Pillars & Principles

1. **Portfolio-First Discovery**: Actual visual work matters more than marketing descriptions or purchased social media followers.
2. **Verified Trust**: Triple-tier verification distinguishing government identity verification, business GST/license verification, and authentic booking completion verification.
3. **Transparent Comparison**: Customers can compare rates, deliverables, turnaround times, and camera/drone gear side-by-side.
4. **Fair & Neutral Ranking**: Paid subscriptions never secretly manipulate organic search recommendations.
5. **Local Relevance**: Location radius, travel availability, and regional shooting nuance matter deeply.
6. **AI-Assisted, Human-Controlled**: AI suggests and extracts structured constraints; customers and professionals make the final decisions.
7. **Zero Vendor Lock-in & Free Software First**: Zero paid developer software subscriptions or mandatory paid AI APIs required to develop and operate the core platform.

---

## 3. Product Triad

* **Product 1 — Customer Marketplace**: Search, filter, shortlist, compare, enquire, negotiate, book, pay, and review professionals.
* **Product 2 — Professional Business SaaS**: Identity profile, portfolio manager, lead inbox, quotation generator, calendar availability, and business analytics.
* **Product 3 — AI Intelligence Platform**: Natural-language requirement parsing, visual portfolio classification, 7-factor weighted candidate recommendation, and verified review summarization.
